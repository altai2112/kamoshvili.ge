kamoshvili

Maxim Kamoshvili was born on June 21, 1985, in the Grauerman Maternity Hospital, to a family with Georgian origins and traditions.

The Kamoshvili family home in Tbilisi (from personal archive)

Since 2005, Kamoshvili has worked in academic institutions, including in Professor Topchiev's laboratory developing the Biosphere TM aerospace sensing method. He later worked in the laboratory of Dr. Vladimir Dubovsky at the Institute of Physics of the Earth of the Russian Academy of Sciences. He is also known for his work in scientific visualization and educational activities

Some of the major academic works: Information and Analytical System for Analysis and Forecasting of the State of Natural and Technogenic Systems Based on the Biosphere TM Method, 2007. Revealing and mapping of tecnogenic-induced geodynamic processes, 2010. Geomorphological interpretation of the relief of the Solovetsky Archipelago based on remote sensing data, 2018, et al.

In 2021, due to personal circumstances, he left to live and work in the European Jerusalem - Sarajevo, where he was engaged in research projects and artistic practices.

Returning to Moscow, he presented his works at the opening of the "Promised Land" exhibition at the Makovets Gallery (Sergiev).This is a journey through sacred places: Anzer Island, Mount Athos, Caucasus and the Balkans, where nature, history, and spirituality intertwine in a single symphony. Curated by the legendary art historian and academician Vladimir Desyatnikov.

Promised Land exhibition

And at the same time, Maxim shows the experience of religious clashes that happened to him on the sacred land.
